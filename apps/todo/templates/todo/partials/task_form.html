{% load todo_ui %} {% comment %} The task form, in two modes. `mode='quick'`: the quick-add box (title, date, time, priority, labels; posts to todo:create and re-renders the list in `view`). `mode='details'`: the task page's details (the same controls without a title; saves `part=details` of the task). The details have no Save button: they save themselves on every change (`data-autosave`, static/todo/js/tasks.js); the labels and repeat popovers save when they close. A save swaps nothing itself (`hx-swap="none"`): the script picks out of the answered panel only the parts the change touched (`hx-select-oob`: `#task-details-fields`, `#task-repeat-line`, `#task-reminders`, `#task-msg`), so an editor or menu open elsewhere on the page stays open, and the form with its status line stays in place. Needs `labels` (the user's) and `selected_label_ids`; in details mode `task`. Both modes read dates out of text with the AI (static/todo/js/title_parse.js, the `data-title-parse` containers): the title of the quick-add box, and the "Type a date" box of the details. {% endcomment %} {% if mode == 'quick' %}
