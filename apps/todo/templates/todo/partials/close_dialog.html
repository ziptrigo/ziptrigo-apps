{% comment %} The close dialog: what Complete (a parent with open subtasks) and "Close as won't do" ask. Opened with `todoOpenCloseDialog(button)` (close_dialog.js) from a button carrying `data-url`, `data-mode` (`done` or `wont_do`), `data-ctx` (`row` or `page`: where the answer goes), `data-title` (the task) and `data-count` (its open subtasks), `data-blockers` (how many open blockers it has) and `data-blockers-url` (where to fetch their names). A task with open blockers asks to confirm: the form posts `confirm_blockers=1` and lists them; so does closing subtasks (not "leave them open") that have open blockers of their own, which it names too. A plain POST, like the confirmation modal: the views redirect back (a refusal is a flash message). Won't do has an optional note, which becomes a comment; with open subtasks it asks whether to close them as Done, as Won't do, or leave them open. {% endcomment %}
