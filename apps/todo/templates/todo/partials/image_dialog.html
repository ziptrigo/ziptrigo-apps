{% comment %} The image popup (image_dialog.js): the full image of an attachment, over the same page. Opened by a click on a thumbnail (`a[data-attachment-thumbnail]`, `attachment_thumbnail.html`); the script sends `open-image-dialog` with the image's `src` and the link's `href`. Escape, a click outside the image or Close close it. "Open in new tab" is the same link the row's **Open** has. A modified click on a thumbnail (Ctrl, Cmd, Shift, middle button) skips the popup and opens the link. {% endcomment %}
