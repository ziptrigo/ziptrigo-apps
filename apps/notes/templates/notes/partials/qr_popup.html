Its only control is Close, so Tab stays on it; Escape and a click on the backdrop close it and focus goes back to the button. `@keydown.tab.prevent` on the panel relies on Close being the only focusable control inside it: if another control is ever added, replace it with a real focus trap. Self-contained: its own `x-data`.{% endcomment %}
